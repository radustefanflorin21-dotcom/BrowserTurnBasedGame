DUNGEON: THE VERDANT DEEP
REGION: Heart of Gaia
RECOMMENDED LEVEL: 36-43
ROOMS: 6
DUNGEON TYPE: Group dungeon
KEY: Verdant Deep Key
KEY CONSUMED ON ENTRY: Yes
MAIN THEMES: jungle growth, healing pressure, poison, bleed, evasion, living vines, ancient green magic.

============================================================
1. ENTRANCE NPC
============================================================
NPC NAME: Nali Rootwatcher
ROLE: Dungeon entrance NPC
LOCATION: Heart of Gaia, beside an overgrown jungle fissure surrounded by giant leaves and glowing vines.

NPC VISUAL DESCRIPTION:
Nali Rootwatcher is a young but serious jungle sentinel wearing woven leaf armor, vine straps, and a cloak made from layered green feathers. Her skin and clothes are stained with pollen and sap, and her hair is braided with small flowers, jungle seeds, and animal charms. She carries a curved wooden spear and a satchel filled with medicinal herbs. Her expression is calm but guarded, as if she hears the jungle speaking constantly.

NPC DIALOGUE - HAS KEY:
"The roots below are older than the trees above. They remember every wound Gaia ever took. If you carry the Verdant Deep Key, I can open the vine seal. Step carefully. The jungle does not hate you, but it may still decide to swallow you."
Options:
- Use Verdant Deep Key: consumes key and enters dungeon.
- Leave: closes dialogue.

NPC DIALOGUE - NO KEY:
"No key? Then the vines will not part. They only open for those carrying a living mark of the jungle. Bring me a Verdant Deep Key, and try not to bleed on the flowers while finding one. They get excited."

NPC DIALOGUE - AFTER BOSS:
"The deep roots are quiet... quieter than I have ever heard them. You faced the old bloom and came back. Come, before the jungle decides it misses you."
Options:
- Take me out: exits dungeon.
- I will stay longer: closes dialogue.

KEY VISUAL DESCRIPTION:
The Verdant Deep Key looks like a living vine grown into the shape of a key. Its handle is a loop of twisted green stems wrapped around a glowing seed. The shaft is dark heartwood with small leaves growing from it, and the teeth are made from thorn tips and polished animal bone. Faint golden-green sap pulses inside the wood, and tiny roots move slowly along the surface.

============================================================
3. DUNGEON MECHANICS
============================================================
MECHANIC: Overgrowth Snare
Starts in Room 3. Every 3 rounds, one random player has a 35% chance to receive Cripple for 1 turn. Cripple increases stamina cost by +1.

MECHANIC: Jungle Bloom
Starts in Room 4. Every 4 rounds, the lowest-HP enemy receives a small heal equal to 5% max HP. This should not affect bosses if normal enemies are alive; it should support adds first.

BOSS ROOM MECHANIC: Root Reinforcements
In Room 6 only, if the fight lasts too long:
- Round 8: summon 1 Verdant Sprout
- Round 12: summon 1 Verdant Sprout
Do not summon if there are already 8 enemies alive.

============================================================
4. ROOM STRUCTURES
============================================================
ROOM 1: Moss-Covered Descent
Enemies: Greenleaf Squirrel x2, Greenleaf Parrot x2, Greenleaf Fox x1, Jungle Stag x1
Total enemies: 6
Visuals: A damp jungle tunnel descending through mossy stone and thick roots. Shafts of green light enter from holes above. Bright insects, wet leaves, and glowing sap drops cover the walls. It should feel alive and humid, but not yet hostile.

ROOM 2: Canopy Rootbridge
Enemies: Greenleaf Squirrel x2, Greenleaf Parrot x2, Greenleaf Fox x2, Jungle Stag x1
Total enemies: 7
Visuals: A suspended natural bridge made from enormous roots, crossing a shadowed jungle ravine. Vines hang from above, tropical flowers grow along the edges, and distant animal calls echo below. The bridge should feel beautiful but dangerous.

ROOM 3: Hunter's Green
Enemies: Greenleaf Fox x2, Greenleaf Squirrel x2, Jungle Stag x1, Greenleaf Parrot x1, Gorilla x1
Total enemies: 7
Visuals: A wide clearing filled with clawed trees, trampled flowers, and broken branches. The ground is soft and covered in footprints. The room feels like a predator gathering place, with hanging vines forming natural ambush points.

ROOM 4: The Blooming Watch
Elite room 1.
Enemies: Jungle Stag x2, Greenleaf Parrot x2, Greenleaf Squirrel x1, Greenleaf Fox x1, Gorilla x1, Verdant Bloomseer x1
Total enemies: 8
Visuals: A sacred jungle chamber with massive flowers blooming from ancient roots. Pools of glowing green sap reflect hanging vines. The air is full of pollen and drifting golden spores. This room should feel magical and healing-focused.

ROOM 5: Primal Canopy Gate
Elite room 2.
Enemies: Gorilla x2, Greenleaf Fox x2, Jungle Stag x1, Greenleaf Parrot x1, Greenleaf Squirrel x1, Primordial Silverback x1
Total enemies: 8
Visuals: A heavy gate of bent trees, cracked stone, and vine-covered bones. Huge handprints mark the walls. Broken trunks and crushed roots show where something massive has forced its way through many times.

ROOM 6: Heartbloom Cradle
Boss room.
Enemies: Greenleaf Squirrel x1, Greenleaf Parrot x1, Greenleaf Fox x1, Jungle Stag x1, Gorilla x1, Verdant Bloomseer x1, Primordial Silverback x1, The Heartbloom Ancient x1
Total enemies: 8
Visuals: A huge underground jungle cathedral centered around a glowing heart-shaped flower grown from ancient roots. The floor is covered in moss, flowers, and pulsing vine veins. The ceiling is hidden by leaves and roots. This is the living heart of the dungeon.

BOSS ROOM PHASE VISUALS:
Phase 1 - Sleeping Bloom: The heart-flower glows gently, vines move slowly, pollen floats peacefully.
Phase 2 - The Jungle Closes: At 70% HP, roots rise, flowers open, green sap brightens, and the room becomes tighter and more aggressive.
Phase 3 - Gaia's Pulse: At 35% HP, the heart-flower cracks open with intense green light, vines whip violently, and spores fill the air like a storm.

5. ELITE AND BOSS MONSTERS
============================================================
ELITE 1: VERDANT BLOOMSEER
Level: 40
Rarity: Epic Elite
Role: Support/Controller
Stamina: 7
HP: 980
Stats: STR 24, DEX 52, VIT 74, INT 112

Visual Description:
A tall deer-bird jungle spirit with leaf-feather wings, mossy antlers, and glowing flower clusters growing from its shoulders. Its eyes shine green-gold, and its body looks woven from vines, feathers, and bark. It should feel wise, graceful, and dangerous through healing magic rather than brute force.

Skills:
- Bloom Mend: Cooldown 3. Heals lowest-HP ally for VIT x 85%. If target is below 35% HP, also grants Healing received +8% for 2 turns.
- Pollen Blind: Cooldown 3. Targets all players. Base accuracy 88%. 45% chance to apply Accuracy down -8% for 2 turns.
- Thorned Bloom: Cooldown 2. Magical damage INT x 50%. Base accuracy 86%, crit 3%. 45% chance to apply Poison, 12% dealt damage per turn, 2 turns.
- Greenward Song: Cooldown 4. All allies gain Magic resist +6% and Status resist +5% for 2 turns.
- Basic Attack: Magical, INT x 35%, base accuracy 90%, crit 2%.

ELITE 2: PRIMORDIAL SILVERBACK
Level: 41
Rarity: Epic Elite
Role: Bruiser
Stamina: 7
HP: 1080
Stats: STR 92, DEX 40, VIT 70, INT 22

Visual Description:
A huge ancient gorilla covered in bark-like armor plates, moss, vines, and pale jungle scars. Its arms are massive, with knuckles like tree roots and stone. Its eyes glow amber under a heavy brow. It should feel like a living jungle titan, built for crushing and protecting territory.

Skills:
- Canopy Breaker: Cooldown 2. Physical damage STR x 105%. Base accuracy 82%, crit 5%. 40% chance to apply Phys resist down -8% for 2 turns.
- Ground Roar: Cooldown 3. Target + 1 adjacent. Physical damage STR x 65%. 35% chance to apply Cripple for 1 turn.
- Primal Guard: Cooldown 4. Self gains Phys resist +8% and Phys dmg +8% for 2 turns.
- Rootknuckle Slam: Cooldown 4. Physical damage STR x 85%. Base accuracy 80%, crit 4%. 18% chance to Stun for 1 turn.
- Basic Attack: Physical, STR x 60%, base accuracy 88%, crit 4%.

BOSS: THE HEARTBLOOM ANCIENT
Level: 43
Rarity: Myth Boss
Role: Support/Bruiser
Stamina: 8
HP: 3600
Stats: STR 78, DEX 60, VIT 112, INT 126

Visual Description:
A colossal ancient tree-beast grown around a glowing heart-shaped flower. Its body is a fusion of bark plates, vines, moss, and animal bones absorbed into the roots. It has long branch-like arms and a chest cavity glowing with green-gold sap. It should feel like Gaia's old heart defending itself.

Boss Phases:
Phase 1 - Sleeping Bloom, 100%-70% HP: normal rotation.
Phase 2 - The Jungle Closes, triggers at 70% HP: boss gains Healing received +8% and Magic resist +8% permanently. Summons 1 Greenleaf Parrot and 1 Jungle Stag.
Phase 3 - Gaia's Pulse, triggers at 35% HP: boss gains Magic dmg +10% and Accuracy +8% permanently. Overgrowth Snare triggers every 2 rounds instead of every 3. Basic attacks hit target + 1 adjacent.

Boss Skills:
- Heartroot Pulse: Cooldown 3. Target all allies. Heals VIT x 55%. Also grants Status resist +5% for 1 turn.
- Vinecrush: Cooldown 2. Single target physical damage STR x 100%. Base accuracy 82%, crit 5%. +15% final damage if target has Cripple.
- Sporefall: Cooldown 4. All players. Magical damage INT x 45%. 45% chance Poison, 12% dealt damage per turn, 2 turns. 35% chance Accuracy down -6%, 1 turn.
- Ancient Barkskin: Cooldown 4. Self gains Damage reduction 15% and Status resist +8% for 2 turns.
- Blooming Rupture: Cooldown 5. Phase 2+. Target + 2 adjacent. Magical damage INT x 55%. 40% chance Magic resist down -8% for 2 turns.
- Gaia Heartbreak: Cooldown 5. Phase 3 only. All players. Magical damage INT x 55%. 18% chance Stun for 1 turn.
- Basic Attack: Physical, STR x 65%, base accuracy 88%, crit 4%. Phase 3: target + 1 adjacent, STR x 55% per target.

BOSS SUMMON: VERDANT SPROUT
Level: 36
Role: Support summon
HP: 240
Stats: STR 14, DEX 26, VIT 34, INT 42
Skills: Sprout Mend heals lowest ally VIT x 45%, cooldown 3. Thorn Flick magical INT x 30%, 30% Poison chance for 1 turn.
