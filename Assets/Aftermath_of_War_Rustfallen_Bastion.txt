DUNGEON: THE RUSTFALLEN BASTION
REGION: Aftermath of War
RECOMMENDED LEVEL: 36-43
ROOMS: 6
DUNGEON TYPE: Group dungeon
KEY: Rustfallen Key
KEY CONSUMED ON ENTRY: Yes
MAIN THEMES: ruined battlefield, rust, ash, ghosts, old weapons, armor corrosion, damage suppression, summons, war echoes.

============================================================
1. ENTRANCE NPC
============================================================
NPC NAME: Captain Ilyra Voss
ROLE: Dungeon entrance NPC
LOCATION: Aftermath of War, beside a collapsed fortress gate half-buried in ash and broken weapons.

NPC VISUAL DESCRIPTION:
Captain Ilyra Voss is a battle-worn veteran wearing dented half-plate, a torn red-gray cloak, and a cracked officer's gorget. She is not undead, but she looks haunted by everything around her. Her hair is tied back roughly, her face is scarred, and her eyes are tired but alert. She carries an old sword with a broken tip, more symbol than weapon, and keeps a bundle of rusted dog tags at her belt.

NPC DIALOGUE - HAS KEY:
"That key opens a grave that should have stayed shut. The Bastion still thinks the war is happening. If you enter, it will draft you into a battle that ended years ago. Hand me the Rustfallen Key, and I will open the gate."
Options:
- Use Rustfallen Key: consumes key and enters dungeon.
- Leave: closes dialogue.

NPC DIALOGUE - NO KEY:
"No Rustfallen Key, no entry. Good. That place has enough dead heroes. Bring the key if you insist on becoming another bad idea in armor."

NPC DIALOGUE - AFTER BOSS:
"The marching stopped. For the first time, the Bastion is silent. You ended something old in there. Come back to the living side of the gate."

============================================================
KEY NAME: Rustfallen Key
TYPE: Dungeon key
PROFESSION: Provisioner
REQUIRED CRAFTING LEVEL: 38
RECIPE SLOTS: 8

KEY RECIPE:
1. Rusted Plate Scrap x6
2. Ashen Cloth x5
3. Wraith Dust x6
4. Broken Spearhead x5
5. Warbone Fragment x4
6. Corroded Core x4
7. Faded Banner Thread x4
8. Fallen Echo Core x2

KEY VISUAL DESCRIPTION:
A black iron key eaten by rust, wrapped in a strip of faded battlefield cloth. Its teeth are shaped like broken spearheads, and a small ghostly ember glows inside the handle. The key should look recovered from a mass grave, ancient and cursed rather than ornate.

============================================================
3. DUNGEON MECHANICS
============================================================
MECHANIC: Rust Cloud
Starts in Room 3. Every 3 rounds, one random player has a 35% chance to receive Phys damage down -6% for 1 turn.

MECHANIC: War Echo
Starts in Room 4. Every 4 rounds, one random player has a 30% chance to receive Accuracy down -6% for 1 turn.

BOSS ROOM MECHANIC: Fallen Reinforcements
Room 6 only:
- Round 8: summon 1 Fallen Echo
- Round 12: summon 1 Fallen Echo
Do not summon if there are already 8 enemies alive.

============================================================
4. ROOM STRUCTURES
============================================================
ROOM 1: Ashen Gatehouse
Enemies: Ash Horror x2, Cinder Husk x2, Ash Skulker x1, Remnant of Rust x1
Total enemies: 6
Visuals: A ruined gatehouse filled with ash drifts, broken shields, rusted chains, and collapsed stone. Faint red light leaks through cracks in the walls. The air feels smoky and old.

ROOM 2: The Broken Barracks
Enemies: Ash Horror x2, Cinder Husk x2, Ash Skulker x2, Remnant of Rust x1
Total enemies: 7
Visuals: A collapsed soldiers' barracks with rotted bunks, torn banners, smashed helmets, and ghostly silhouettes burned into the walls. Ash lies over everything like snow.

ROOM 3: Rustmarch Yard
Enemies: Remnant of Rust x2, Ash Skulker x2, Cinder Husk x1, Ash Horror x1, Faded War Wraith x1
Total enemies: 7
Visuals: A wide courtyard of cracked stone, rusted weapons, old siege debris, and piles of armor. The ground is stained dark from ancient battles. The room should feel like a battlefield that never fully ended.

ROOM 4: The Corroded Armory
Elite room 1.
Enemies: Remnant of Rust x2, Cinder Husk x2, Ash Horror x1, Ash Skulker x1, Faded War Wraith x1, Rustbound Marshal x1
Total enemies: 8
Visuals: A long armory filled with rusted blades, broken shields, armor racks, and chains. Some weapons tremble on the walls as if remembering combat. Orange rust dust floats in the air.

ROOM 5: The Wraith Parade
Elite room 2.
Enemies: Faded War Wraith x2, Remnant of Rust x2, Ash Skulker x1, Ash Horror x1, Cinder Husk x1, Bannerless Wraithlord x1
Total enemies: 8
Visuals: A ceremonial war hall where torn banners hang from high pillars. Ghostly footprints march across the dusty floor. Old drums, broken standards, and skeletal horses line the sides.

ROOM 6: The Last Command
Boss room.
Enemies: Ash Horror x1, Cinder Husk x1, Ash Skulker x1, Remnant of Rust x1, Faded War Wraith x1, Rustbound Marshal x1, Bannerless Wraithlord x1, The Last Warmaster x1
Total enemies: 8
Visuals: A ruined command chamber with a cracked war table at the center. Broken maps, rusted swords, and fallen banners surround a throne-like command seat. The walls are covered with old battle plans and burn marks.

BOSS ROOM PHASE VISUALS:
Phase 1 - Orders Remembered: The room is dim, with ghostly whispers and faint marching echoes.
Phase 2 - The War Returns: At 70% HP, spectral banners rise, weapons rattle, and fallen soldiers appear from ash.
Phase 3 - No Surrender: At 35% HP, the chamber burns with red ghost-light, rust storms swirl, and the floor shakes with marching dead.

5. ELITE AND BOSS MONSTERS
============================================================
ELITE 1: RUSTBOUND MARSHAL
Level: 40
Rarity: Epic Elite
Role: Controller/Tank
Stamina: 7
HP: 1050
Stats: STR 62, DEX 36, VIT 82, INT 58

Visual Description:
A heavily armored undead commander fused with rusted plate and broken chains. Its helmet has no visible face, only a dim ember glow inside. Rust flakes fall from it as it moves. It carries a corroded command blade and looks like a walking piece of ruined military authority.

Skills:
- Corrode Command: Cooldown 3. All players. Base accuracy 88%. 45% chance Both damage down -6% for 2 turns.
- Rusted Guard: Cooldown 4. Self gains Phys resist +10% and Status resist +6% for 2 turns.
- Marshal's Cleave: Cooldown 2. Physical STR x 85%, target + 1 adjacent. 40% chance Phys resist down -6% for 2 turns.
- Chain Order: Cooldown 4. Single target. Base accuracy 86%. 45% chance Cripple for 2 turns.
- Basic: Physical STR x 55%, accuracy 88%, crit 3%.

ELITE 2: BANNERLESS WRAITHLORD
Level: 41
Rarity: Epic Elite
Role: Summoner/Mage
Stamina: 7
HP: 940
Stats: STR 22, DEX 44, VIT 64, INT 112

Visual Description:
A floating war ghost wrapped in shredded banners and spectral armor fragments. Its body is mostly smoke and ash, with a hollow helm and glowing pale eyes. Torn flags drift from its back like broken wings. It should feel tragic, commanding, and dangerous.

Skills:
- Call Fallen: Cooldown 4. Summons 1 Fallen Echo if enemy count allows.
- Soul Chill: Cooldown 3. All players. Magical INT x 40%. 40% chance Accuracy down -6% for 2 turns.
- Banner Curse: Cooldown 3. Single target. 45% chance Magic resist down -8% for 2 turns.
- Haunting Bolt: Cooldown 2. Magical INT x 60%, accuracy 86%, crit 3%. 35% chance Status resist down -5% for 2 turns.
- Basic: Magical INT x 35%, accuracy 90%, crit 2%.

BOSS: THE LAST WARMASTER
Level: 43
Rarity: Myth Boss
Role: Bruiser/Summoner
Stamina: 8
HP: 3700
Stats: STR 118, DEX 54, VIT 108, INT 72

Visual Description:
A towering undead general in shattered black armor, covered in rust, ash, and spectral red light. A broken crown-like helm sits on its head, and a massive two-handed sword is chained to its wrist. Its body is partly physical and partly ghostly, as if the war refuses to let it die.

Boss Phases:
Phase 1 - Orders Remembered, 100%-70%: normal rotation.
Phase 2 - The War Returns, 70%: gains Phys resist +8% and summons 1 Remnant of Rust and 1 Faded War Wraith.
Phase 3 - No Surrender, 35%: gains Phys dmg +10%, Accuracy +8%, and Fallen Reinforcements trigger every 3 rounds.

Boss Skills:
- Commanding Ruin: Cooldown 3. All players. Base accuracy 86%. 45% chance Both damage down -8% for 2 turns.
- Warmaster's Execution: Cooldown 2. Physical STR x 105%. +20% final damage if target below 40% HP.
- Ruststorm Slash: Cooldown 4. Target + 2 adjacent. Physical STR x 70%. 40% chance Phys resist down -8% for 2 turns.
- Raise the Fallen: Cooldown 5. Summons 1 Fallen Echo, max 3 active.
- No Retreat: Cooldown 4. Self gains Phys dmg +8% and Status resist +8% for 2 turns.
- Final Order: Cooldown 5, Phase 3 only. All players. Physical STR x 55%. 18% chance Stun for 1 turn.
- Basic: Physical STR x 65%, accuracy 88%, crit 4%. Phase 3 hits target + 1 adjacent at STR x 55%.

BOSS SUMMON: FALLEN ECHO
Level: 36
Role: Summon bruiser
HP: 260
Stats: STR 44, DEX 26, VIT 36, INT 18
Skills: Echo Strike physical STR x 45%. Broken March has 30% chance Cripple for 1 turn.
