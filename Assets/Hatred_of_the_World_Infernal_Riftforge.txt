3. DUNGEON ENTRANCE VISUAL DESCRIPTION
============================================================
The entrance to The Infernal Riftforge sits at the base of a black volcanic cliff where the ground has split open into a glowing wound. The gate is not cleanly built; it is a brutal forge-door made from obsidian slabs, warped iron, cooled magma, and chains that have partly melted into the stone.

A vertical crack runs through the center of the door, leaking orange-red light and waves of heat distortion. Around it, old forge symbols are carved into the rock, but many have been burned away or cracked by pressure. Pools of cooling lava glow between plates of black slag. Ash falls constantly, even when the sky is clear.

The entrance should feel aggressive and oppressive. Unlike nature dungeons that feel alive, this place feels like anger given architecture. The mountain does not breathe; it grinds. The sound from beyond the gate is a mixture of distant hammering, roaring fire, breaking stone, and something huge dragging metal across the floor.

Important visual elements:
- volcanic cliff wall
- massive obsidian forge gate
- warped iron chains
- glowing red-orange crack through the door
- black ash falling from above
- cooled lava crust and slag pools
- heat distortion
- molten runes
- Kael Ashbrand standing to one side
- Riftforge Key slot glowing like a furnace wound

Mood:
- violent
- oppressive
- volcanic
- furious
- ancient forge
- dangerous heat
- hatred made physical

5. DUNGEON MECHANICS
============================================================
MECHANIC: Heat Surge
Starts in Room 3 and continues in Rooms 3-6.
Every 3 rounds, one random player has a 35% chance to receive Burn for 2 turns.
Burn tick: 4% of the affected player's max HP per turn.
This is an ambient dungeon Burn and does not use dealt-damage scaling.

Skill-based Burn remains separate:
- Inferno Gaze Burn: 12% of dealt damage per turn for 3 turns.
- Ember Forgeling Spark Spray Burn: 10% of dealt damage per turn for 2 turns.
- Ember Forgeling Meltdown Burn: 8% of dealt damage per turn for 1 turn.

NO SECOND ROOM DEBUFF:
The old Molten Floor / Fragile room mechanic is intentionally removed. Heat Surge is the only persistent dungeon-wide debuff mechanic. This keeps the dungeon simpler and avoids stacking too many passive penalties with boss burns.

BOSS ROOM MECHANIC: Forge Eruptions
In Room 6 only:
- Round 8: summon 1 Ember Forgeling if fewer than 8 enemies are alive.
- Round 12: summon 1 Ember Forgeling if fewer than 8 enemies are alive.
Ember Forgelings count toward the 8-enemy cap.
Maximum active Ember Forgelings: 2.

Boss room starts with 7 enemies so the round 8 Forgeling can spawn unless another summon has filled the slot.

BOSS PHASE 2 SUMMON:
At 70% HP, The Riftforge Tyrant summons 1 Ember Scuttler and 1 Ash Lizard once, if fewer than 8 enemies are alive.
If only one slot is open, summon in priority order:
1. Ember Scuttler
2. Ash Lizard

BOSS PHASE 3 HEAT SURGE:
At 35% HP, Heat Surge frequency changes from every 3 rounds to every 2 rounds.
This replaces the normal Heat Surge interval. It does not stack with the every-3-round version.

8. NORMAL MONSTER VISUAL IDENTITIES
============================================================
ASH LIZARD
A low, lean volcanic reptile with charcoal-black scales, ember-red eyes, and cracked glowing lines along its back. Its belly and throat glow faintly like banked coals. It should look quick, heat-adapted, and vicious, with burnt hide stretched over sharp ribs and small smoking claws.

CINDER STALKER
A thin, predatory fire-beast that moves through smoke and ash. It has long limbs, ember-lit joints, blackened fur or hide, and burning fang tips. Its silhouette should feel like an assassin: low, fast, and hard to see through heat haze.

EMBER SCUTTLER
A small crab/spider-like volcanic creature with a shell made from cooled ember-rock. Its many legs tap against stone, and its back glows with a round Ember Core. It should feel annoying, twitchy, and control-focused, with heat vents releasing sparks from its shell.

MAGMA BOAR
A heavy boar with thick Magma Hide, glowing tusk roots, and lava-like cracks across its shoulders. It should look aggressive and durable, with smoke pouring from its nostrils and hooves that leave glowing prints on the floor.

LAVA BASILISK
A dangerous volcanic basilisk with molten scales, a crown of obsidian spikes, and one or more glowing Basilisk Eyes. It should look magical and controlling rather than purely physical. Its stare should feel hot enough to petrify flesh into glassy slag.
