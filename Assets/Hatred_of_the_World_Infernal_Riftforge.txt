DUNGEON: THE INFERNAL RIFTFORGE
REGION: Hatred of the World
RECOMMENDED LEVEL: 48-55
ROOMS: 6
DUNGEON TYPE: Group dungeon
KEY: Riftforge Key
KEY CONSUMED ON ENTRY: Yes
MAIN THEMES: lava, volcanic rage, burn, reflect, armor melt, physical burst, destructive boss phases.

============================================================
1. ENTRANCE NPC
============================================================
NPC NAME: Kael Ashbrand
ROLE: Dungeon entrance NPC
LOCATION: Hatred of the World, beside a cracked obsidian forge gate pouring smoke and orange light.

NPC VISUAL DESCRIPTION:
Kael Ashbrand is a burned but stubborn forge exile wearing soot-black leather, cracked metal plates, and a hood scorched at the edges. His arms are wrapped in heat-resistant cloth and old chain. One side of his face is marked by healed burn scars, and his eyes reflect firelight. He carries a heavy iron hammer and a ring of blackened keys.

NPC DIALOGUE - HAS KEY:
"That gate is not locked to keep us out. It is locked because something inside still wants to finish burning the world. Give me the Riftforge Key, and I will open it. Quickly. The metal gets angry when it waits."

NPC DIALOGUE - NO KEY:
"No Riftforge Key? Then keep your eyebrows and walk away. The forge only opens for fire-marked steel, and fools with empty hands become smoke."

NPC DIALOGUE - AFTER BOSS:
"The forge roared, then choked. You broke its hatred for now. Come out before the mountain remembers how to breathe fire."

============================================================
3. DUNGEON MECHANICS
============================================================
MECHANIC: Heat Surge
Starts in Room 3. Every 3 rounds, one random player has a 35% chance to receive Burn for 2 turns. Burn deals 10% of the triggering heat damage equivalent per turn.

MECHANIC: Molten Floor
Starts in Room 4. Every 4 rounds, one random player has 30% chance to receive Fragile for 1 turn, causing them to take extra flat damage from incoming hits.

BOSS ROOM MECHANIC: Forge Eruptions
Room 6 only:
- Round 8: summon 1 Ember Forgeling
- Round 12: summon 1 Ember Forgeling
Do not summon if 8 enemies are already alive.

4. ROOM STRUCTURES
============================================================
ROOM 1: Ashen Intake
Enemies: Ash Lizard x2, Cinder Stalker x2, Ember Scuttler x1, Magma Boar x1
Total enemies: 6
Visuals: A black volcanic tunnel with vents of smoke, cooling lava rivers, and glowing ash on the floor.

ROOM 2: The Burning Chainworks
Enemies: Ash Lizard x2, Cinder Stalker x2, Ember Scuttler x2, Magma Boar x1
Total enemies: 7
Visuals: A ruined forge hall full of hanging chains, molten hooks, and red-orange light reflected on black stone.

ROOM 3: Scuttler Furnace
Enemies: Ember Scuttler x2, Ash Lizard x2, Cinder Stalker x1, Magma Boar x1, Lava Basilisk x1
Total enemies: 7
Visuals: A furnace chamber of broken pipes, lava pools, and metal plates heated red-hot.

ROOM 4: Basilisk Kiln
Elite room 1.
Enemies: Lava Basilisk x2, Ember Scuttler x2, Ash Lizard x1, Cinder Stalker x1, Magma Boar x1, Molten Oracle x1
Total enemies: 8
Visuals: A circular kiln full of petrified molten statues and fire-lit smoke. The walls look glazed by repeated heat.

ROOM 5: The Rage Anvil
Elite room 2.
Enemies: Magma Boar x2, Cinder Stalker x2, Lava Basilisk x1, Ember Scuttler x1, Ash Lizard x1, Ashmaw Titan x1
Total enemies: 8
Visuals: A giant anvil platform above lava, cracked by impacts. Huge hammer marks and splashes of molten metal cover the floor.

ROOM 6: The World-Hate Forge
Boss room.
Enemies: Ash Lizard x1, Cinder Stalker x1, Ember Scuttler x1, Magma Boar x1, Lava Basilisk x1, Molten Oracle x1, Ashmaw Titan x1, The Riftforge Tyrant x1
Total enemies: 8
Visuals: A colossal volcanic forge built around a lava rift. Chains, obsidian pillars, molten channels, and a furnace-heart dominate the arena.

BOSS ROOM PHASE VISUALS:
Phase 1 - Banked Hatred: Lava glows low, chains hang still, forge-heart pulses slowly.
Phase 2 - Furnace Unchained: At 70% HP, chains snap, lava channels overflow, sparks storm through the room.
Phase 3 - Worldfire: At 35% HP, the rift tears open, the forge-heart burns white-orange, and the arena looks close to melting.

5. ELITE AND BOSS MONSTERS
============================================================
ELITE 1: MOLTEN ORACLE
Level: 52
Rarity: Epic Elite
Role: Controller/Mage
Stamina: 7
HP: 1450
Stats: STR 32, DEX 60, VIT 82, INT 150

Visual Description:
A lava basilisk prophet with molten eyes, black glass scales, and a crown of burning stone horns. Its body is long and low, but its head is raised like a priest of fire. Lava glows under its cracked hide.

Skills:
- Inferno Gaze: Cooldown 2. Magical INT x 60%. 45% Burn chance, 12% dealt damage per turn, 3 turns.
- Petrifying Heat: Cooldown 4. Magical INT x 45%. 18% Stun chance, 1 turn.
- Melt Defenses: Cooldown 3. Single target. 45% Both resists down -8% for 2 turns.
- Ember Prophecy: Cooldown 4. All allies gain Magic dmg +8% for 2 turns.
- Basic: Magical INT x 40%, accuracy 90%, crit 3%.

ELITE 2: ASHMAW TITAN
Level: 53
Rarity: Epic Elite
Role: Bruiser
Stamina: 7
HP: 1650
Stats: STR 150, DEX 54, VIT 96, INT 35

Visual Description:
A towering magma boar/titan hybrid with cracked black armor plates and lava glowing between them. Its tusks are molten stone, its shoulders are huge, and every breath releases sparks and ash.

Skills:
- Molten Charge: Cooldown 2. Physical STR x 110%. 40% Fragile chance, 1 turn.
- Boiling Rage: Cooldown 3. Self gains Phys dmg +10% for 2 turns.
- Ashbreaker Slam: Cooldown 4. Target + 1 adjacent. Physical STR x 70%. 35% Cripple chance, 1 turn.
- Lava Armor: Cooldown 4. Self gains Phys resist +8% and reflect 10% for 2 turns.
- Basic: Physical STR x 60%, accuracy 88%, crit 4%.

BOSS: THE RIFTFORGE TYRANT
Level: 55
Rarity: Myth Boss
Role: Bruiser/Controller
Stamina: 8
HP: 5200
Stats: STR 170, DEX 70, VIT 135, INT 105

Visual Description:
A colossal demon-forge beast made of obsidian armor, molten veins, and living hatred. Its torso resembles a furnace, its arms are huge smithing hammers, and its head is crowned with broken volcanic horns. It should look like a boss born from lava and rage.

Boss Phases:
Phase 1 - Banked Hatred, 100%-70%: normal rotation.
Phase 2 - Furnace Unchained, 70%: gains Phys dmg +8% and Magic dmg +8%, summons 1 Ember Scuttler and 1 Ash Lizard.
Phase 3 - Worldfire, 35%: gains Accuracy +8%, Heat Surge triggers every 2 rounds, basic attacks hit target + 1 adjacent.

Boss Skills:
- Tyrant's Hammer: Cooldown 2. Physical STR x 105%. +15% final damage if target is Fragile.
- Riftflame Breath: Cooldown 3. All players. Magical INT x 45%. 45% Burn chance for 2 turns.
- Molten Sentence: Cooldown 4. Single target. 45% Both damage down -8% for 2 turns and 35% Fatigue for 1 turn.
- Forgehide: Cooldown 4. Self gains Damage reduction 15% and Status resist +8% for 2 turns.
- Worldfire Eruption: Cooldown 5, Phase 2+. Target + 2 adjacent. Hybrid damage STR x 45% + INT x 35%. 40% Burn chance.
- Hatred Unbound: Cooldown 5, Phase 3 only. All players. Magical INT x 55%. 18% Stun chance for 1 turn.
- Basic: Physical STR x 65%. Phase 3 target + 1 adjacent at STR x 55%.

BOSS SUMMON: EMBER FORGELING
Level: 48
Role: Bruiser summon
HP: 320
Stats: STR 64, DEX 36, VIT 42, INT 24
Skills: Ember Punch physical STR x 45%. Spark Spray 30% Burn chance for 1 turn.
