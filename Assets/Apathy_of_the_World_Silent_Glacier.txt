DUNGEON: THE SILENT GLACIER
REGION: Apathy of the World
RECOMMENDED LEVEL: 48-55
ROOMS: 6
DUNGEON TYPE: Group dungeon
KEY: Silent Glacier Key
KEY CONSUMED ON ENTRY: Yes
MAIN THEMES: deep frost, silence, emotional emptiness, evasion, poison/frostbite pressure, stun, mitigation, cold attrition.

============================================================
1. ENTRANCE NPC
============================================================
NPC NAME: Maera Stillvoice
ROLE: Dungeon entrance NPC
LOCATION: Apathy of the World, beside a frozen crevasse covered by pale blue mist.

NPC VISUAL DESCRIPTION:
Maera Stillvoice is a quiet arctic guide wrapped in layered white furs, gray cloth, and frost-covered leather. Her lips and eyelashes are pale with cold, and she speaks very little. She carries a bone lantern filled with blue flame and a long ice pick. Her expression is calm in a frightening way, as if fear froze out of her years ago.

NPC DIALOGUE - HAS KEY:
"The glacier is listening. It does not hate. It does not hunger. It simply waits until you stop moving. Give me the Silent Glacier Key, and I will open the crevasse. Do not waste warmth inside."

NPC DIALOGUE - NO KEY:
"No key. No passage. That is mercy. The glacier keeps what enters without purpose. Bring the Silent Glacier Key if you still wish to be remembered by no one."

NPC DIALOGUE - AFTER BOSS:
"The silence cracked. I heard it. Come back now, before it closes around your name."

============================================================
3. DUNGEON MECHANICS
============================================================
MECHANIC: Numbing Silence
Starts in Room 3. Every 3 rounds, one random player has a 35% chance to receive Accuracy down -6% for 1 turn.

MECHANIC: Frozen Joints
Starts in Room 4. Every 4 rounds, one random player has a 30% chance to receive Cripple for 1 turn.

BOSS ROOM MECHANIC: Ice Reflections
Room 6 only:
- Round 8: summon 1 Frost Reflection
- Round 12: summon 1 Frost Reflection
Do not summon if 8 enemies are already alive.

============================================================

Enemies: Icy Mink x2, Icy Serpent x2, Glacier Turtoise x1, Frozen Stalker x1
Total enemies: 6
Visuals: A narrow glacier tunnel of blue-white ice, with muffled sound and faint frost mist.

Enemies: Icy Mink x2, Icy Serpent x2, Glacier Turtoise x2, Frozen Stalker x1
Total enemies: 7

ROOM 3: Whiteout Hunting Ground
Enemies: Frozen Stalker x2, Icy Mink x2, Icy Serpent x1, Glacier Turtoise x1, Frost Skitter x1
Total enemies: 7
Visuals: A wide snow-filled chamber where visibility fades into mist. Shadows move behind ice walls.

ROOM 4: Crystal Nerve Web
Elite room 1.
Enemies: Frost Skitter x2, Icy Serpent x2, Glacier Turtoise x1, Icy Mink x1, Frozen Stalker x1, Crystal Webmother x1
Total enemies: 8
Visuals: A chamber filled with fine ice webs, crystal threads, frozen cocoons, and faint blue nerve-like light.

ROOM 5: The Hollow Shell Crossing
Elite room 2.
Enemies: Glacier Turtoise x2, Frozen Stalker x2, Frost Skitter x1, Icy Serpent x1, Icy Mink x1, Glacial Shellwarden x1
Total enemies: 8
Visuals: A frozen bridge through huge turtle-shell-like ice formations and hollow glacier domes.

ROOM 6: The Empty Eye
Boss room.
Enemies: Icy Mink x1, Icy Serpent x1, Glacier Turtoise x1, Frozen Stalker x1, Frost Skitter x1, Crystal Webmother x1, Glacial Shellwarden x1, The Apathy Below x1
Total enemies: 8
Visuals: A circular glacier chamber with a dark frozen lake at the center, reflecting the ceiling like an empty eye.

BOSS ROOM PHASE VISUALS:
Phase 1 - Still Surface: The lake is calm, ice blue, and silent.
Phase 2 - The Ice Remembers: At 70% HP, cracks spread under the lake and pale reflections appear.
Phase 3 - Nothing Answers: At 35% HP, the room dims, frost mist thickens, and the lake becomes black and mirrorlike.

5. ELITE AND BOSS MONSTERS
============================================================
ELITE 1: CRYSTAL WEBMOTHER
Level: 52
Rarity: Epic Elite
Role: Controller
Stamina: 7
HP: 1420
Stats: STR 34, DEX 82, VIT 82, INT 132

Visual Description:
A huge frost spider-like creature made of glassy ice, white fur, and crystal nerves. Its legs are long and thin but armored with ice edges. Its abdomen glows faintly with frozen blue light. It should feel precise, alien, and coldly intelligent.

Skills:
- Ice Web: Cooldown 2. Magical INT x 50%. 45% Cripple chance for 2 turns.
- Crystal Nerves: Cooldown 4. Self gains Evasion +15% and Status resist +8% for 2 turns.
- Absolute Thread: Cooldown 4. Single target. Magical INT x 45%. 18% Stun chance for 1 turn.
- Frost Poison: Cooldown 3. Magical INT x 45%. 45% Poison chance, 12% damage per turn for 3 turns.
- Basic: Magical INT x 35%, accuracy 90%, crit 2%.

ELITE 2: GLACIAL SHELLWARDEN
Level: 53
Rarity: Epic Elite
Role: Tank
Stamina: 7
HP: 1780
Stats: STR 58, DEX 26, VIT 150, INT 42

Visual Description:
A gigantic glacial tortoise with a shell like a frozen cathedral dome. Its shell is layered with blue ice plates, white frost, and dark stone trapped inside. It moves slowly, leaving deep tracks in snow and ice.

Skills:
- Glacier Shell: Cooldown 3. Self gains Phys resist +10% and Magic resist +10% for 2 turns.
- Shell Challenge: Cooldown 4. All players. 55% Taunt chance for 1 turn. Self gains Damage reduction 12% for 1 turn.
- Slow Crush: Cooldown 2. Physical STR x 90%. 40% Cripple chance for 1 turn.
- Icequake Step: Cooldown 4. Target + 1 adjacent. Physical STR x 65%. 16% Stun chance for 1 turn.
- Basic: Physical STR x 50%, accuracy 88%, crit 2%.

BOSS: THE APATHY BELOW
Level: 55
Rarity: Myth Boss
Role: Controller/Tank
Stamina: 8
HP: 5400
Stats: STR 88, DEX 70, VIT 150, INT 150

Visual Description:
A vast frozen spirit emerging from a black glacial lake. Its upper body is humanoid but faceless, made of translucent ice, frozen roots, and dark water trapped inside. It should feel empty rather than angry. Its eyes are barely visible, like pale lights far beneath ice.

Boss Phases:
Phase 1 - Still Surface, 100%-70%: normal rotation.
Phase 2 - The Ice Remembers, 70%: gains Magic resist +8% and Status resist +8%, summons 1 Icy Serpent and 1 Frost Skitter.
Phase 3 - Nothing Answers, 35%: gains Accuracy +8%, Numbing Silence triggers every 2 rounds, and basic attacks hit target + 1 adjacent.

Boss Skills:
- Hollow Frost: Cooldown 3. All players. Magical INT x 45%. 45% Accuracy down -8% for 2 turns.
- Empty Grasp: Cooldown 2. Single target magical INT x 60%. 40% Cripple chance for 2 turns.
- Black Ice Shell: Cooldown 4. Self gains Damage reduction 16% and Status resist +8% for 2 turns.
- Memory Freeze: Cooldown 5, Phase 2+. Single target. Magical INT x 55%. 20% Stun chance for 1 turn.
- Frozen Reflection: Cooldown 4. Target + 2 adjacent. Magical INT x 50%. 40% Magic damage down -8% for 2 turns.
- Absolute Silence: Cooldown 5, Phase 3 only. All players. Magical INT x 55%. 18% Stun chance for 1 turn.
- Basic: Magical INT x 40%. Phase 3 target + 1 adjacent at INT x 35%.

BOSS SUMMON: FROST REFLECTION
Level: 48
Role: Controller summon
HP: 320
Stats: STR 18, DEX 42, VIT 40, INT 70
Skills: Reflection Shard magical INT x 35%. 30% Accuracy down for 1 turn. Vanish Ice gives Evasion +12% for 1 turn.
