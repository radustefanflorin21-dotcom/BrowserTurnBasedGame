ENEMY SKILLS REFERENCE
Browser Turn-Based Game — generated from config.js + combat scripts
================================================================================

Each enemy has a combat role, spawn rarity, and a scripted ability rotation.
Bosses (isBoss) and dungeon elites often have phase transitions at ~70% and ~35% HP.

HOW ENEMY DAMAGE IS CALCULATED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Enemy attack (ATK) scales with level, rarity stat budget, and role stat split.
Skill hit ≈ floor(ATK × skill multiplier × outgoing damage modifiers × mood/region).
Some skills use monsterPhysicalDamageFromBase(level-scaled) instead of a flat ATK %.

Example damage below uses ATK ≈ 45 (typical mid-level foe), BEFORE
player resist, buffs/debuffs, and crit. Actual values vary by level and rarity.

Enemy stamina: common 6, rare 7, epic 7, myth 8, ancient 9 (per turn budget).
Cooldown (CD): turns until the ability can be used again after casting.

COMBAT ROLES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
tank         High VIT, physical resist; taunts, shields, mitigation.
bruiser      STR-focused; heavy single-target and AoE hits.
assassin     DEX-focused; burst damage, bleed, evasion.
mage         INT-focused; magic damage and DoTs.
controller   Disrupts with blind, cripple, accuracy down, debuff extension.
support      Heals allies, party buffs, light damage.
summoner     Spawns reinforcements, buffs minions.
